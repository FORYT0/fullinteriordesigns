What we do

Eight services, one site team

Every job — inside or outside the house — moves through the same design, quote and install process, so finishes stay consistent from the living room ceiling to the porch soffit. Already have drawings from your own architect or designer? We build to those too.

Interior Design

Space planning, material and colour selection, and 3D concepts for living rooms, bedrooms, kitchens and offices.

Gypsum Ceilings

Flat, tray and stepped gypsum ceilings, bulkheads and coffers — designed to carry your lighting layout.

Gypsum Feature Walls

TV walls, headboard walls and panel detailing in gypsum, shaped to carry hidden wiring and light fittings.

Recessed & Mirror Lighting

Recessed downlights, LED cove lighting in gypsum steps, and backlit mirrors and wall panels.

Wardrobe Assemblies

Built-in and walk-in wardrobes, sliding and hinged door systems, internal shelving, drawers and LED-lit hanging space.

Kitchen & Cabinet Fit-Outs

Kitchen cabinetry, dressing tables and vanity units, TV and display units, dining wall units, office wardrobes and pantry cabinets.
