About us

9+ years of gypsum, lighting & interior fit-outs in Kenyan homes

Full Interior Designs Kenya builds the detail that makes a room feel finished — ceilings, feature walls, lighting and the wardrobes and cabinetry around them — for homeowners across the country.

How we work

Two ways to start a project

  • Our own design, built by us

    We visit, measure and design the space ourselves — gypsum layout, lighting plan, wardrobe and cabinet fit-outs — then fabricate and install it with our own site team, start to finish.

  • Built to your architect's design

    Already have drawings from your own architect or designer? We work directly from those, matching the specification and finish you've already signed off on.

What we specialise in

Gypsum, lighting & the cabinetry-heavy rooms

Our core strength is gypsum design work on fixture walls and ceilings, paired with lighting — behind mirrors, on fixture walls, and recessed into gypsum steps and coves. Around that, we handle the rooms that live or die on their cabinetry: wardrobe assemblies, kitchen cabinetry, dressing tables, TV and display units, dining wall units, office wardrobes and pantry cabinets. Renovations and exterior finishing — soffits, fascia, cladding, porch lighting — follow the same design-and-build process.
